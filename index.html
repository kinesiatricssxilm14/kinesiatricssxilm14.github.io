👨‍💻 About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student (Direct MS-to-PhD program) at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ), advised by Professor Cuiyun Gao. I am expected to graduate in 2029.

My research focuses on Software Engineering, LLM Agents, and AI Coding. I am excited to explore AI Coding at every level—from models and agents to benchmarks, tools, and developer workflows—and warmly welcome research collaborations.

Concurrently, I am participating in the Rhino-Bird Elite Talent Program at WeChat Pay, Tencent WXG, working on agent-related tasks.

My MBTI type is ENFP.🐕

Stay passionate about life, always. ✨
